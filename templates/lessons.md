# Lessons

_Append-only, and the last resort. A lesson is written only when nothing cheaper stops the mistake recurring — a check that goes red, or a standing rule (the lesson ladder, `logging.md › Drift and lessons`). Things that went wrong in how work was done — drift, over-building, scope left, a wrong assumption acted on — and what to do instead. Agents read the entries in force at the start of every piece of work, next to the constitution. Newest at the bottom. Nothing is deleted: when an entry's retire condition arrives it gets a `Retired:` line saying when and why, and is no longer read._
